## currently cooking 🍳

### [MYDAY — every day has a story. find yours.](https://www.myday.buzz)

A public collection of meaningful dates and the people behind them.

- 📅 Register a date for free, or make a paid featured claim.
- ✍️ Give it a title, a story, and a public tag.
- 🏆 Explore featured dates ranked by claim value.
- 🔁 A higher valid paid claim can take the featured spot.

Not legal ownership of a date. Just a little corner of the internet for days that matter.

**The interesting bits:** responsive UI, public records, server-checked prices, hosted checkout, and signed payment webhooks.

> The browser saying “success” isn't enough. The webhook gets the final word.
